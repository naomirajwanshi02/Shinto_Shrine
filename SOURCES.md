# Sources

The 3D model is an original procedural drawing based on the supplied illustration (the tracking picture) and on
general descriptions of Shinto shrine architecture. Nothing was copied or downloaded into the project.

Facts used in `js/data.js`:

- Torii, komainu, temizuya, haiden, shimenawa and shide, ema, toro:
  [Shinto architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto_architecture),
  [Insider's Guide to Shinto Shrines (nippon.com)](https://www.nippon.com/en/views/b05201/your-virtual-guide-to-the-shinto-shrine.html),
  [20 Words You Need to Know Before Visiting a Shinto Shrine (Tsunagu Japan)](https://tsunagujapan.com/en/japanese-culture-explained-shinto-shrines)
- Torii parts (hashira, kasagi, shimaki, nuki, kusabi, gakuzuka):
  [Torii (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torii), [JAANUS: Inari torii](https://www.aisf.or.jp/~jaanus/deta/i/inaritorii.htm)
- Inari foxes, red bibs, items held in the mouth or paws: [Inari shrine (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inari_shrine),
  [Everything about the Inari fox (Tsunagu Japan)](https://www.tsunagujapan.com/everything-you-ve-wanted-to-know-about-the-inari-fox-japan-s-cutest-sacred-animal/)
- Mikoshi, phoenix finial, carrying poles: [Mikoshi (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikoshi)
- Prayer manners (coin, two bows, two claps, one bow) and the meaning of "ema" (horse picture) are standard,
  widely documented customs.

Hall sides and back (veranda round the whole building, railing with top, middle and bottom rails, cypress-bark roof, hall placed in front of
a deeper sanctuary): [Shinto architecture (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto_architecture),
[JAANUS: haiden](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/haiden),
[JAANUS: koran](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/45),
[Shinto Architecture (World History Encyclopedia)](https://www.worldhistory.org/Shinto_Architecture/),
[Main Gate, Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto Travel)](https://ja.kyoto.travel/komafuda/show.php?id=2262&lang=en).
The window, door and post layout on the far end and back follows the front and long side of the supplied picture.
